Beauty & wellbeing · Figma prototype, mobile & desktop

Ô Natur'Elle — A complete prototype for a beauty salon

Ô Natur'Elle is a premium beauty salon founded by a qualified beautician. For this real client project, I designed a complete, responsive Figma prototype from start to finish, with one guiding idea: help an undecided client find the right treatment and book it effortlessly — treatment showcase, personalised beauty diagnosis, 3-step booking, shop and gallery.

  • Scope: 30+ screens, in mobile and desktop versions
  • Key feature: a 4-question diagnosis that recommends the right treatment
  • Flow: 3-step booking with an animated confirmation
Role: UI/UX DesignerClient: qualified beautician, BelgiumTools: Figma

02
Local retail · Figma prototype + working code, mobile

Belvantage — a shared loyalty card for independent shops

A personal project, thought through end to end: a single app lets customers collect points across several different local shops, without any shopkeeper having to build anything. Taken beyond the Figma prototype into working HTML/CSS/JS code, with a real QR code and a real camera scanner.

  • Scope: 41 screens/states in Figma, 20 coded pages
  • Key decision: switched from a 4-digit code to the QR code as the main flow, after benchmarking
  • Beyond design: a QR code and camera scanner that really work, dark mode without duplicated screens
Role: UI/UX Designer & DeveloperContext: personal projectTools: Figma, HTML/CSS/JS

Graphic project

Art direction & visual identity

Alongside UI/UX, I also work on visual identity. For Ô Natur'Elle, I drew the logo myself: a single-line portrait of the beautician, designed to embody the softness and elegance of the brand. Further down you will also find posters, an ad visual and a logo.

Brand identity · Ô Natur'Elle

The Ô Natur'Elle logo — a hand-drawn portrait

Rather than starting from an abstract monogram, I chose to draw the beautician's own face as a single continuous line (single line art), surrounded by floral motifs that echo the salon's natural treatments. The circular stroke and the script lettering of "Ô natur'elle" balance the precision of the portrait with softness.

  • Type: real logo, client project (Ô Natur'Elle)
  • Technique: single-line illustration, drawn by hand
  • Use: brand identity, applied to the website and the Figma prototype
Awareness poster · Violence against women

“STOP à la violence contre les femmes” — letting the image speak

A high-contrast black and white photo: a woman with her eyes closed, a man's hand resting on her face. The message fits in one word. The capitalised “STOP”, tilted on the edge of the poster, cuts into the image like a gesture of interruption, and the line that follows (“stop violence against women”) rises diagonally towards the face.

  • Type: awareness poster, personal project
  • Format: landscape
  • Design choices: black and white, tilted typography, text kept to a minimum
Awareness poster · Comic book style

“Cœur à cœur, pas poing contre poing.” — a pun that hits home

Same cause, different tone: a comic-style illustration in red and brown tones shows an injured woman. The slogan (“heart to heart, not fist against fist”) plays on the near-rhyme of cœur / poing and sits in white letters on a black band shaped like a speech bubble, so it reads in a second.

  • Type: awareness poster, personal project
  • Format: portrait
  • Design choices: warm palette, capital lettering, black speech-bubble band
Ad visual · Lime Funérarium

Lime Funérarium — talking about grief gently

A delicate subject handled without gloom: a grandfather and his grandson walk past a sunlit house, and the brand's lime green fills the foreground. The slogan, in calligraphic italics (“life ends, but the freshness of memories remains”), links the end of life to memories that stay “fresh”, a nod to the brand's name.

  • Type: ad visual, personal project
  • Format: square, suited to social media
  • Design choices: the brand's lime green, calligraphic typography, a bright family scene
Logo · Hair salon

The rabbit in the hat — a logo that makes you smile

A rabbit pops out of a magician's hat, alongside a comb and scissors: the idea of a “magic” transformation, paired with hairdressing tools. All in black and white, with a crisp line that stays legible at small and large sizes.

  • Type: logo for a hair salon, personal project
  • Colours: black and white
  • Design choices: the magician symbol, comb and scissors built into the drawing
